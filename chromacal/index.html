ChromaCal

Auto-color Google Calendar™ by keyword.

Write a rule once — “Standup → blue”, “Deadline → red” — and every matching event gets a real Google Calendar color that also shows on your phone.

1. Make rules

Pick a keyword (or a regular expression) and a color. Rules higher in the list win.

2. Review

See exactly which events will change before anything is written. Events you colored yourself are kept.

3. Apply

ChromaCal sets the event colors in your Google Calendar. Undo the last run with one click, or let new events be colored automatically every 30 minutes.

What ChromaCal accesses

ChromaCal uses the official Google Calendar API, directly from your browser, only after you connect your Google account:

  • Your calendar list (names and colors) — so you can choose which calendars to color.
  • Calendar color labels — Google Calendar stores custom event colors as labels on each calendar; ChromaCal adds the labels your rules need.
  • Event titles, times and current colors on calendars you own — to find events that match your rules and set their color.

ChromaCal never reads event descriptions, attendees, or locations, never creates, moves or deletes events, and has no server: your data goes only between your browser and Google. See the privacy policy.
