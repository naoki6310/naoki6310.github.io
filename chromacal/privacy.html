ChromaCal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

ChromaCal is a browser extension that colors events in your Google Calendar™ according to keyword rules you create. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is used.

Data the extension accesses

When you connect your Google account, ChromaCal uses Google's Calendar API to:

  • list the calendars you own (names, ids and colors), so you can choose which ones to color;
  • read the events in the date range you choose (title, start time, recurrence, and current color label) to find events that match your rules;
  • add color labels to your calendars and set the color label of matching events.

ChromaCal does not read event descriptions, attendees, locations, attachments or any other calendar content, and it never creates, deletes or reschedules events.

Where data goes

Nowhere except Google. The extension talks directly from your browser to Google's Calendar API. The developer runs no server and never receives your calendar data, your rules, or your account details. Nothing is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.

What is stored, and where

  • Your rules and settings are stored with chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome syncs to browsers signed in to your own Google account.
  • To support “keep colors I picked myself” and undo, the extension stores on your device only (chrome.storage.local) the ids of events it colored, the ids of labels it created, the previous color label of events changed in the last run, and your Google account's numeric id (used to refresh sign-in without asking again).
  • The short-lived Google access token is kept in memory for the browser session (chrome.storage.session) and is never written to disk by the extension.

Uninstalling the extension removes this stored data. You can disconnect inside the extension at any time, and you can revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ChromaCal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
